PROJECT BRIEF

ABOUT THE
PROJECT

MiamiMuscle Imports is a German automotive import service that sources American muscle cars directly from the United States and delivers them, fully registered, to clients across Deutschland. They approached Glory Design Studio for a single-page website that could match the adrenaline of the product itself.

The core requirement was a visually stunning, scroll-driven experience powered by Three.js — an interactive car delivery sequence that walks the visitor from a shipping container dropping off an ocean vessel, across the Atlantic, to the moment a custom-painted Dodge Challenger rolls out onto German soil. All wrapped in a bold retrowave aesthetic of neon grids, hot pink, and Miami sunsets.

04 / KEY FEATURE

THREE.JS
SCROLL ANIMATION

The centerpiece of the site is a scroll-triggered 3D scene rendered in real time with Three.js. As the visitor scrolls, the camera guides them through a seven-stage cinematic delivery: an intro reveal, a shipping container dropping from the cargo vessel, the back doors swinging open, the Dodge Challenger rolling out, and the final hand-over on German soil — all set against a glowing retrowave environment.
